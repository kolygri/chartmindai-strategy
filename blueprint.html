Basic valid workflow · UK B2B SaaS

Reconcile recognised revenue from contract to ledger.

A deliberately narrow monthly-close workflow for fixed-fee SaaS subscriptions recognised evenly over time. It separates accounting revenue from billing, cash, VAT and ARR—and sends anything outside the approved policy to a qualified finance reviewer.

01 · Valid operating boundary

Start with one accounting pattern.

This workflow is valid only when the customer has already approved the accounting policy and the contracts fit the simple pattern below. The product applies that policy; it does not invent accounting conclusions.

Included

Simple fixed-fee subscriptions

  • One UK legal entity and GBP functional currency.
  • Approved and enforceable customer contracts.
  • One distinct subscription service.
  • Fixed consideration excluding VAT.
  • Service transferred evenly over a stated term.
  • No material collectability concern at contract inception.
Controller review required

Do not automate in v1

  • Bundled implementation or professional services.
  • Usage-based or other variable consideration.
  • Mid-term modifications, concessions or side letters.
  • Multiple entities, currencies or principal-agent questions.
  • Refunds, complex credits, financing or collectability issues.
  • Any contract that does not match the approved policy.
Critical distinctionARR and NRR may appear in the management brief, but they are not recognised revenue. Billing creates a receivable and may create a contract liability; cash settles a receivable; revenue follows satisfaction of the subscription performance obligation.
02 · One-time policy setup

Accounting policy comes before automation.

The Financial Controller signs the revenue policy and defines the population the system is permitted to process. For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2026, revised FRS 102 Section 23 uses a five-step model based on IFRS 15.

STEP 1

Identify contract

Approved agreement, identifiable rights and payment terms, commercial substance and probable collection.

STEP 2

Identify obligation

One distinct stand-ready subscription service within this deliberately simple boundary.

STEPS 3–4

Price and allocate

Fixed price excluding VAT; 100% allocated to the single subscription obligation.

STEP 5

Recognise

Revenue over time using the approved daily or monthly measure of progress.

Policy record

Framework, effective date, service pattern, day-count convention, cut-off rule, materiality and approver.

Eligibility rule

Machine-testable conditions defining which contracts may enter the automated population.

Change control

A policy or eligibility change creates a new version and cannot alter an already approved close.

03 · Minimum source set

Three current exports plus the prior approved reconciliation.

The earlier “CRM, billing and P&L” language is too loose for revenue accounting. These four slots define the basic authoritative evidence chain.

01 · CONTRACT REGISTER

Rights and service term

Contract ID, customer, status, signed date, start/end dates, fixed price, obligation type and amendment flag.

02 · BILLING REGISTER

Invoices and credits

Invoice/credit ID, contract ID, issue and tax-point date, net value, VAT, gross value and currency.

03 · GENERAL LEDGER

Recorded balances

Revenue, trade receivables, contract assets and contract liabilities by account, period and journal ID.

P−1 · APPROVED CLOSE

Opening control totals

Prior closing schedules, unresolved items, approved adjustments and reconciliation sign-off.

Source authorityThe contract register supports recognition rules; billing supports invoices and VAT; the GL is the statutory book of record; the previous approved close supplies controlled opening balances. The prior management pack alone is not sufficient accounting evidence.
04 · Recurring monthly workflow

Eight gated steps from intake to sign-off.

Every step identifies the finance action, automated action, resulting record and condition that stops the close.

01

Open the revenue-close period

Choose legal entity, month, policy version and named preparer and approver.

Gate: scope locked

Finance action

Controller confirms period, materiality, policy and GL accounts in scope.

System action

Freezes the policy version and imports prior approved closing totals.

Output

Controlled close ID with accountable users and opening balances.

Stop when

Policy is draft, previous close is unapproved or required accounts are missing.

02

Load and validate the four source slots

Preserve originals and prove the files belong to the period and entity.

Gate: intake complete

Finance action

Uploads exports, records extraction time and confirms source completeness.

System action

Hashes files; tests required columns, duplicates, dates, currency and contract IDs.

Output

Immutable intake manifest and data-quality report.

Stop when

A slot is absent, a file is unreadable, the period is wrong or a material field is missing.

03

Establish the eligible contract population

Only simple contracts matching the approved policy enter the automated schedule.

Gate: population approved

Finance action

Reviews new, ended or flagged contracts and assigns complex items to the Controller.

System action

Tests signed status, dates, fixed price, single obligation and modification flags.

Output

Included population, excluded population and reason per contract.

Stop when

An included contract fails eligibility or a material contract has no accounting treatment.

04

Build the revenue schedule

Calculate revenue by contract for the current period under the locked policy.

Gate: schedule reproducible

Finance action

Reviews the schedule summary and period cut-off; does not overwrite calculated values.

System action

Excludes VAT, allocates fixed consideration and recognises the current-period service amount.

Output

Contract-level revenue schedule with formula, policy version and contributing source rows.

Stop when

Dates overlap, price is negative, allocation is incomplete or the run cannot be reproduced.

05

Reconcile schedule, GL and balance sheet

Explain recognised revenue and the related contract balances.

Gate: differences within tolerance

Finance action

Reviews differences by contract, account and journal; assigns every unresolved item.

System action

Runs revenue-to-GL, contract-liability, contract-asset and receivables rollforwards.

Output

Reconciliation ledger: expected, recorded, difference, owner and status.

Stop when

An unexplained difference exceeds materiality or opening balances do not match P−1.

06

Resolve exceptions and prepare journals

Correct the source or propose an evidenced accounting adjustment.

Gate: no blockers

Finance action

Chooses source correction, policy review or proposed journal and documents rationale.

System action

Re-runs dependent controls, preserves before/after values and drafts—but never posts—the journal.

Output

Resolved exception log and proposed journal with evidence links.

Stop when

A material item is open, evidence is absent or preparer and approver are the same person.

07

Post approved journal and re-reconcile

Record the approved adjustment in the accounting system, then prove the books agree.

Gate: final GL imported

Finance action

Controller approves; authorised accountant posts externally and exports the final GL.

System action

Imports final balances, reruns all controls and checks journal ID and amount.

Output

Final zero or explained difference and linked posted-journal reference.

Stop when

The journal is unapproved, posting differs from proposal or final GL remains unreconciled.

08

Review, sign and freeze the close

Separate preparation from accountable accounting approval.

Gate: controller sign-off

Finance action

Preparer completes checklist; Controller samples contracts, journals and rollforwards.

System action

Locks the run, hashes outputs and records identities, timestamps and comments.

Output

Approved revenue reconciliation, schedules, journal evidence and audit manifest.

Stop when

A review point remains open or any source, policy, schedule or journal changed after submission.

05 · Minimum reconciliation set

Four proofs make the close coherent.

These formulae are simplified control equations. Account names may differ, but each movement must be supported by contract, invoice or journal evidence.

Revenue schedule → GLΣ revenue recognised by eligible contract ± approved adjustments = revenue recorded in the GL
Contract liabilityOpening liability + billings/receipts before performance − revenue released ± approved adjustments = closing liability
Contract assetOpening asset + revenue before unconditional billing − transfers to receivables ± impairment/adjustments = closing asset
Trade receivablesOpening receivables + invoices − cash − credits/write-offs ± FX = closing receivables
Separate VAT controlVAT follows tax-point and invoice rules, which may not match accounting-revenue timing. The billing register must preserve net, VAT and gross values, but the revenue schedule uses consideration excluding VAT. VAT-account reconciliation remains a separate close control.

08 · Ownership and segregation

Commercial ownership does not replace accounting authority.

Head of FP&A / VP Finance

Owns the close outcome, scope, operator capacity, business adoption and commercial continuation decision.

Revenue accountant / FP&A operator

Loads evidence, reviews populations, investigates differences and prepares proposed journals.

Financial Controller

Owns revenue policy, complex accounting conclusions, materiality, journal approval and close sign-off.

ChartMindAI

Executes the approved policy, calculates schedules, enforces gates and preserves evidence. It does not post autonomously.

Minimum segregationThe person preparing an adjustment should not be its final approver. The FP&A business owner may own the product and process outcome, but the Controller owns statutory accounting conclusions.
09 · Approved close pack

The final artifact is an accounting control package.

Core reconciliation

Control summary

Schedule-to-GL result, balance rollforwards, materiality, exceptions, final status and signatories.

Supporting schedules

Traceable detail

Eligible contracts, excluded items, contract-level revenue, invoices, GL lines and posted journals.

Audit manifest

Evidence and history

Source hashes, policy version, formulae, run IDs, changes, approvals and retained export files.

RetentionVAT records generally must be retained for at least six years and relevant records must remain digitally linked where Making Tax Digital applies. The customer’s broader statutory and audit retention policy still governs the complete close package.
