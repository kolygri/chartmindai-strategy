Competitive reviewWhat not to build

The category is ahead of the old thesis

Trusted finance workflows are already a product category.

Current vendor and open-source products remove “semantics + evidence + approval + MCP” as a credible near-term differentiator. Their feature claims are vendor evidence—not proof of customer outcomes—but they clearly define the competitive baseline.

DefensibilityRight to learn

No moat yet

Earn a right to learn before claiming defensibility.

Models, MCP, charting, semantic layers and audit logs are increasingly available from platforms and open source. Customer approval history is also private and not automatically portable. The first asset is a repeatable workflow and its evaluation set—not “workflow memory.”

Evidence to earn in 90 days

  • Two matched customers complete two live cycles
  • One reusable schema covers most of both implementations
  • A golden test pack catches material regressions
  • Customers renew because the outcome—not the founders—is valuable

Channel and table stakes

  • MCP is supported by ChatGPT apps and Microsoft Copilot Studio
  • Charts, PPTX, prompt skills, hosting, SSO and audit logs
  • Generic semantic or context layers available via open source

Highest-risk assumptions

Test what can kill the strategy before polishing it.

These are unresolved. Each has a concrete early test and a stop condition.

01 · DEMAND

The brief is not urgent enough

Monthly reporting may be painful but tolerable, especially when spreadsheets already work.

Kill if matched users will not share artifacts or make a commercial commitment.
02 · SERVICES

Every pack is different

Definitions, source shapes and commentary may require too much expert tailoring.

Current model reliability makes unchecked finance automation unsafe.

Kill if review time or material errors do not improve versus baseline.

Recommended next move90 days · capacity-capped

The 90-day execution plan

Produce two credible wins—or stop.

Cap work at roughly 30 engineering and 30 commercial hours per week. Preserve the existing MCP and rendering substrate; spend new effort only on evidence required for the next gate.

DAYS 1–15

Recruit and baseline

Commercial: 12–15 matched conversations and five artifact walkthroughs. Engineering: demo only; no new platform work.

DAYS 16–30

Select and contract

Choose the monthly brief only if pain repeats. Secure three complete artifact sets and two commercial commitments. Define one golden test pack.

DAYS 31–60

Deliver cycle one

Run two manual-first pilots with no more than three exports. Measure total preparation, review loops, errors, trace time and bespoke hours.

DAYS 61–90

Repeat and decide

Run cycle two, test reuse across customers and ask for continued paid use. Commit more, revise once or stop against the gates.

Position to validate: “ChartMindAI turns your existing finance exports into a reviewed monthly performance brief—with reconciled KPIs, editable commentary and evidence behind every material number.”
